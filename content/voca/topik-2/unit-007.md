# Level 2 · Elementary · Book 007 — School and work life

- 수업 | class, lesson | sueop | 授業: 수업을 듣다 = take a class; 수업이 끝나다 = class ends | 오늘 수업은 오후 세 시에 끝나요. | Today's class ends at 3 p.m.

- 선배 | senior (at school or work) | seonbae | 先輩: someone who joined before you ↔ 후배 | 선배가 점심을 사 줬어요. | My senior bought me lunch.

- 동료 | coworker, colleague | dongnyo | Pronounced 동뇨 — ㄹ becomes ㄴ after ㅇ | 회사 동료들과 저녁을 먹었어요. | I had dinner with my coworkers.
- 출석 | attendance | chulseok | 出席: 출석을 부르다 = take attendance | 선생님이 수업 전에 출석을 불러요. | The teacher takes attendance before class.
- 결석하다 | to be absent (from class) | gyeolseokada | 缺席: 缺 = missing ↔ 출석하다 | 감기 때문에 학교에 결석했어요. | I missed school because of a cold.
- 지각하다 | to be late | jigakada | 遲刻: late for school or work; 늦다 is general | 버스를 놓쳐서 회사에 지각했어요. | I missed the bus and was late for work.
