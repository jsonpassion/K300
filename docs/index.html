How it works

Five minutes a day. Four steps.

1

Pick your level

Choose your target TOPIK level, or take a 12-question level test. Get hard words right and it places you higher.

2

Flip a book

The front shows only the Korean word. Say it, guess the meaning, then flip for the romanization, meaning, tip and an example.

3

Only the misses again

Missed words collect in one pile. Clear them with cards or the 3-second quiz — they don't leave until you know them.

4

Stack up rounds

Know a word two rounds in a row and it's mastered. Your shelf fills up one book at a time.

Not a word list — a level-by-level shelf

Memory isn't talent. It's how the repetition is designed.

📚

A shelf for each level

Level 1 has 8 books, Level 6 has 12. Spine colors show the level and a red bookmark shows where you are. 100 words and 10 chapters per book.

🔁

Rounds

Finish a book, start round 2, round 3. Go through everything or only what you missed. Two "Know it" rounds in a row and it's mastered.

🎯

Missed words stay put

Say "Don't know" and the word stays until you say "Know it". Clear the pile between chapters or right from the home screen.

⏱️

3-second quiz

If the meaning doesn't come in three seconds, you don't know it yet. The screen border runs down like a clock.

FAQ

Is this an official TOPIK app?

No. TOPIK VOCA is an independent study tool made by ForgeLab. TOPIK is a registered trademark of the National Institute for International Education (NIIED), which administers the test, and this app is not affiliated with, endorsed or approved by NIIED. "TOPIK" appears in the app's name only descriptively, to indicate the exam the content prepares for. All content is original and contains no real test questions.

Why pick a level instead of a score?

TOPIK doesn't give one number on one scale: TOPIK I (Levels 1–2) is scored out of 200 and TOPIK II (Levels 3–6) out of 300, and each level has its own cutoff. So the app lets you choose the level you're aiming for and tracks your estimated score against that level's cutoff. You can switch levels anytime; progress is saved per level.

Why isn't the romanization on the front?

So you read Hangul. The front shows only the Korean word; flip it to see the romanization, English meaning, a tip and an example sentence. Romanization is a crutch for pronunciation, not something to read instead of Korean.

Do I need an account?

No. Your study data is stored in your own iCloud, purchases go through your Apple Account, and nothing is stored on our servers.

What's free?

Book 1 of every level (10 chapters, 100 words), with every feature: rounds, missed-word reviews, the 3-second quiz, Daily 20 and the level test. Books 2 and up need a yearly, monthly or lifetime plan — all three unlock the same content.

How do subscriptions and refunds work?

Yearly and monthly plans renew automatically until you cancel in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions; you keep access until the end of the paid period. Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Refunds follow Apple's policy and can be requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Words are cached on your device after the first download.
